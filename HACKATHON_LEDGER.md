## Operating rules

1. Never misrepresent age, country, school status, identity, project start date, prior work, or team contribution.
2. Do not pay entry fees or accept guardian/legal terms autonomously.
3. Build only against events whose rules permit the work and disclose material AI assistance.
4. Keep the repo public-ready, reproducible, demoable, and timestamped.
5. Prefer real cash + credible judging + low participant count + strong product fit over headline sponsor-credit totals.

## Current build strategy

ContentOps is being built so the same original codebase can serve the creator-workflow target if age eligibility clears, while remaining a legitimate open-theme submission for FirstCommit. No submission is considered registered or submitted until the external platform confirms it.
